Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal desktop integration Tongjin Google Docs CLI, operated by Tongjin. Effective November 18, 2026.

Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorization, the integration accesses Google account identity information, Google document contents and formatting, and metadata or exported contents of Drive files available to the application. Access is used to perform document operations requested by the account holder and to verify the results.

Local Storage and Token Refresh

The desktop CLI stores OAuth credentials locally in encrypted form, with encryption key management provided by the operating system keyring. Refresh tokens are exchanged with Google's OAuth endpoint to obtain access tokens. Local working files may include original document responses, requested changes, verification results, and exports. These files remain until the account holder removes them.

AI-Assisted Operations and Sharing

When the account holder invokes the integration through an AI assistant such as Codex, requested document contents and tool results may be processed by that assistant and its configured model provider as part of the requested task. The account holder should use direct CLI calls when they do not want document content included in an AI conversation. Any separate transfer, such as copying a document to another service, requires an instruction from the account holder.

This personal integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or intentionally use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. AI-provider retention and account data controls are governed by the selected provider's terms and settings; this integration cannot override them. Google user data is used only for the account holder's requested operations.

Limited Use

The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control and Removal

The account holder may stop using the integration, remove local credentials and working files, and revoke its access in Google Account connections. Revoking access does not delete Google documents or previously created local exports. Document deletion or changes to sharing are separate operations.

This Website

This static information site has no sign-in form, document upload facility, or application analytics. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata under its own privacy policy. OAuth authorization and token exchange occur through Google and the local desktop tool, not through this site.
